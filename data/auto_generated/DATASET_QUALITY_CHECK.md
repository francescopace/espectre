## Reading these tables

Every score in these tables combines the shared scale-invariant feature evidence with a temporal-occupancy ceiling; none depends on a detector threshold or probability surface.
- Pair rows keep `Cover` and `Sep` distinct from external static cleanliness (`RefExc` and `RefBurst`). Their final `Score` is capped by both views, so strong separation cannot hide a shifted or persistently active static capture.
- Presence, Empty, and Long-recording rows summarize within-capture stability. Because they use each capture's own center, they can expose bursts and drift but not a uniform cross-session shift.
- Excluded Idle Diagnostics provide that missing cross-session view against admitted references from the same chip, link class, and packet-rate class, preferring the same environment when at least three independent captures are available. Captures with no usable feature rows after temporal admission are listed first with `n/a ⚠️`.
- `Score` is a compact ranking signal only. Admission still turns on integrity, occupancy, continuity, metadata, overlap, and ML readiness.

## Validation rule

- `Stream loss` (missing `stream_seq_num` records): ⚠️ `>1%`, ❌ `>3%` for normal recordings, and ❌ `>5%` when `low_rssi: true`
- `Occ` (mean valid-slot temporal occupancy): ⚠️ `<85%`, ❌ `<70%`; the fail threshold is a dataset-admission gate, and occupancy is a score ceiling
- `Cover` (Pair Scores, motion windows above the idle p95): ⚠️ `<95%`, ❌ `<90%`
- `RefExc` (Pair/Excluded Idle, five-second blocks above the external reference p95): ⚠️ `>25%`, ❌ `>50%`
- `RefBurst` (Pair/Excluded Idle, longest contiguous run above the external reference p99): ⚠️ `>30.0s`, ❌ `>120.0s`
- `n/a ⚠️` (Excluded Idle): no usable feature rows after fixed temporal admission
- `Exc` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording, past median + 3 MAD): ⚠️ `>8%`, ❌ `>13%`
- `Tail` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording, q95 above own median on the feature-evidence axis): ⚠️ `>4.0`, ❌ `>6.0`
- `Sep` (Pair Scores, idle/motion AUC on feature evidence): ⚠️ `<0.990`, ❌ `<0.970`
- `Burst` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording): peer-relative empirical outlier threshold derived from clean idle captures of the same chip in this run; without enough same-chip references, cells fall back to fixed review thresholds
- `Q95` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording): exploratory same-chip signal, shown without soft marks until enough clean references exist
- `Drift` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording): exploratory peer-relative same-chip outlier threshold on the absolute half-to-half median margin drift; no mark is shown when same-chip references are insufficient
- `Score`: 0-100 review ranking only. On pair rows it is the lower of the separation and external-cleanliness composites, capped by the occupancy of every capture in scope. Score remains outside dataset admission; occupancy has its own hard gate.

Computed metrics:

- `Env`: capture environment from `dataset_info.json`
- `File` and `static_presence / motion`: capture-date links to the NPZ paths
- `RSSI`: median per-packet `rssi_dbm`; pair rows show `static_presence / motion`
- `PPS`: observed packets per second from dataset metadata (`num_packets / duration_ms`); pair rows show `static_presence / motion`
- `Occ`: mean valid-slot occupancy across complete production temporal windows; pair rows show `static_presence / motion`
- `Cover` (Pair Scores): share of motion windows whose consensus feature evidence rises above the idle half's own p95
- `Ref`: external-reference scope (`env` or `chip`) and independent capture count
- `RefExc`: share of five-second idle blocks above the external reference p95
- `RefBurst`: longest contiguous five-second idle-block run above the external reference p99
- `Exc` (Presence/Empty/Long-recording): share of windows whose feature evidence exceeds the capture's own median + 3 MAD
- `Sep`: rank-based AUC between idle and motion consensus feature-evidence series
- `Burst`: longest sustained excursion episode in seconds
- `Tail`: 95th percentile of the centered consensus feature-evidence series
- `Drift`: absolute difference between the first-half and second-half median centered feature evidence
- `Score`: indicative 0-100 quality ranking; pair score blends separation, motion coverage, and idle cleanliness, while idle score blends tail cleanliness and burst length; every final score is capped by mean temporal occupancy
