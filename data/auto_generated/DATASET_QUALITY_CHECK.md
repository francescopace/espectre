## Validation rule

- `FP` (Motion Scores): ⚠️ `>5%`, ❌ `>10%`
- `TP` (Motion Scores): ⚠️ `<95%`, ❌ `<90%`
- `FP` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): ⚠️ `>2%`, ❌ `>5%`
- `Ratio` (Motion Scores): peer-relative empirical outlier threshold derived from passing pairs in this run, per chip when enough references exist, otherwise global fallback
- `MAD` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): peer-relative empirical outlier threshold derived from clean idle captures in this run, per chip when enough references exist, otherwise global fallback
- `Burst` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): peer-relative empirical outlier threshold derived from clean idle captures in this run, per chip when enough references exist, otherwise global fallback
- `Score`: absolute 0-100 ranking only; it does not carry peer-relative soft marks

Computed metrics:

- `Env`: capture environment from `dataset_info.json`
- `File` and `static_presence / motion`: capture-date links to the NPZ paths
- `FP` (Motion Scores): share of replayed `ClassicDetector` evaluation ticks classified as motion on `static_presence` (false positives)
- `TP` (Motion Scores): share of replayed `ClassicDetector` evaluation ticks classified as motion on `motion` (true positives)
- `FP` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): `ClassicDetector` false-positive share of evaluation ticks on a self-calibrated idle capture or idle-only quiet test
- `Ratio`: `p95(motion) / threshold` on replayed `ClassicDetector` probabilities
- `MAD` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): robust dispersion of the logit margin `logit(probability) - logit(threshold)` on the post-bootstrap tail
- `Burst` (Presence/Empty/Long-test): longest sustained activation episode in seconds
- `Score`: indicative 0-100 score from `ClassicDetector` replay, tables sorted descending; on Presence/Empty/Long-test it is the self-calibrated idle score (0.5×cleanliness from `FP` + 0.3×stability from `MAD` + 0.2×burst_clean from `Burst`); score is shown as an absolute ranking value without soft review icons
