Embed motion sensing in a connected product

A product with a compatible ESP32 can reuse its Wi-Fi radio to detect nearby movement without adding a camera, microphone, or dedicated motion sensor.

When integration is possible

ESPectre can be embedded when the product firmware can access CSI from a supported ESP32 and provide enough Wi-Fi traffic for continuous sensing.

Compatible ESP32

The device uses an ESP32 family supported by the ESPectre CSI capture and runtime layers.

Usable packet flow

The radio receives a sufficiently regular stream of 2.4 GHz packets in the monitored environment.

Firmware integration

The application can allocate resources for capture and detection, then consume motion events or movement scores.

Products that can react to movement

HVAC and thermostats

Use recent activity as one signal for comfort modes, airflow control, or energy-saving transitions.

Lighting

Let connected bulbs, luminaires, and lighting controllers react locally when movement begins or a room becomes quiet.

Robot vacuums

Schedule, pause, or adapt cleaning behavior when people are moving through the surrounding space.

Air treatment

Give air purifiers, fans, and ventilation equipment another input for activity-aware operating modes.

Displays and appliances

Wake an interface, change standby behavior, or trigger a contextual action when nearby movement is detected.

Building equipment

Add privacy-conscious activity signals to connected controllers, room devices, and distributed automation nodes.

What the product receives

ESPectre turns CSI processing into a small set of signals that can fit naturally into existing product logic.

Motion state

A simple active or inactive state for rules and event triggers.

Movement score

A continuous indication of how strongly the radio environment is changing.

Runtime status

Calibration and diagnostic information for setup, support, and product telemetry.

Wi-Fi sensing is an environmental activity signal. Product behavior should account for placement, room geometry, interference, and the consequences of missed or unexpected detections.

Is the platform a fit?

  • The device uses a compatible ESP32 family with CSI support.
  • It operates on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and receives enough packet traffic for sensing.
  • The firmware can allocate resources for CSI capture and on-device detection.
  • The intended event does not require identity recognition or safety-certified presence detection.
