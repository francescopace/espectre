CSI visualizer

Explore live Wi-Fi Channel State Information through amplitude, phase, and I/Q views. This advanced view pauses normal motion sensing while the signal stream is running.

Inspect the signal behind detection

The CSI visualizer renders amplitude, change, phase, and I/Q views from a temporary stream produced by compatible firmware. It also reports delivery rate, dropped packets, RSSI, SNR, and capture timing.

Starting the stream pauses normal motion sensing until you stop it. Signal data travels directly from the selected device to this browser and is not uploaded or stored by the website.
